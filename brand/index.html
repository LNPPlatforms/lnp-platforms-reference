Logo

The G mark pairs a classic serif letter with three bars rising toward gold: steady growth, built step by step.

PrimaryLight backgrounds
ReversedNavy and dark photos
MarkIcons and avatars

Clear space

Keep empty space around the logo equal to the height of one bar in the mark. Nothing else enters that zone.

Minimum size

The full logo stays at least 120px wide on screen, or 1 inch in print. Below that, use the G mark alone.

06

Voice

How GELP sounds in emails, proposals and on the phone.

SteadyCalm and confident. We've done this before, and it shows.
PlainShort sentences, real numbers, no jargon a provider has to decode.
Partner-firstWe talk about their patients and their goals before our services.

We say

  • "Healthcare providers," which covers solo practitioners up to hospital systems
  • "Here's what it costs and what you get"
  • "We'll handle the outreach"

We avoid

  • "Practices" as a catch-all for every provider
  • "Synergy," "best-in-class," "revolutionary"
  • Promises we can't back with numbers
