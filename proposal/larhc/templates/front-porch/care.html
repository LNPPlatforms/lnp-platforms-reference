Good questions

Frequently asked questions

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. Call us or book online and we'll get you scheduled, usually within a week.

Do you take walk ins?

We save time every morning for same day sick visits. Call first so we can hold a spot for you.

How do I get a prescription refilled?

Request it through the patient portal or ask your pharmacy to send us a request. Please allow two business days.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Your photo ID, insurance card, a list of medicines and any records from your last doctor.
