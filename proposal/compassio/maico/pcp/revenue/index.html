Where the revenue comes from

Three ways it earns its keep.

Every ear complaint

Fevers, ear pain, sick visits and follow-ups. A 10-second test, billed under CPT 92567, that also changes the treatment plan about half the time.1

Fewer repeat visits

Getting the diagnosis right the first time means fewer patients back for a second round of antibiotics that never worked.

Screening days

Host a free kids' ear and hearing screening at a school or daycare. New families find your clinic, and we help you promote it.

Rural health clinic? You may be able to fund the device through the Rural Health Transformation Program. We'll point you to the right application.
