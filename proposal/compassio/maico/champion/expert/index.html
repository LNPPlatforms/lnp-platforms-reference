The role

Three ways a Local Champion leads.

Host screening days

Schools, daycares, churches and health fairs. Kids get checked in minutes, and the ones who need you get referred to you.

Teach local clinics

Lunch-and-learns for nearby pediatric and urgent care teams, built from Dr. LaCour's course. You become the ENT they learned from.

Be the local voice

Featured in the program's local marketing, on the program site and on every flyer in your area.

Why parents show up

Middle ear fluid is common, easy to miss and linked to speech, language and developmental delays.

Why it fills your schedule

Dr. LaCour's experience: of 30 to 40 kids screened, about 8 become patients.1

A sample quarter

Your calendar, planned for you.

We build the schedule with you, then handle the sign-up pages, reminders, flyers and follow-up. You show up and lead.
