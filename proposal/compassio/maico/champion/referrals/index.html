Why it matters

You see what happens when the ear exam guesses wrong.

The kid who got four rounds of antibiotics for fluid that was never infected. The adult whose sudden hearing loss was treated as an ear infection until the treatment window closed. Better tools upstream mean better patients in your chair.

90% / 70%

Primary care felt highly certain in 90% of ear infection diagnoses. Only 70% were true infections.1

3x

The same child is up to three times as likely to be diagnosed and treated depending on which practice they visit.2

97%

A normal Type A tympanogram rules out middle ear fluid 97% of the time, so true nerve loss stands out fast.3
