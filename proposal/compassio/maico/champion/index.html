ENT Local Champion program

Three ways to recruit the local ENT.

Same program, three different pitches. Each page ends with the same short application, and the clinic-facing funnel points referrals to whichever practice signs on.

Version A

Better referrals

"Be the ENT your area's clinics call first." Leads with worked-up referrals, the tympanogram attached and urgent hearing loss caught in time.
Version B

Local expert

"Be the ear expert your community knows by name." Leads with screening days, lunch-and-learns and local visibility.
Version C

The ear care hub

"Be the ear care hub for your area." Leads with being the one champion practice that every nearby clinic refers to, plus a dashboard of those clinics.

Also built: the clinic-facing funnel that sells the easyTymp and training to pediatric, urgent care and family medicine clinics.
