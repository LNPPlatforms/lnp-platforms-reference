What the Collective does

One voice for the clinics rural Louisiana depends on.

Advocate

We speak for Rural Health Clinics with lawmakers, managed care organizations, businesses and the public.

Fund

We manage state and federal funds that strengthen what member clinics can offer their communities.

Inform

Clinic data guides public health strategy across rural Louisiana, so decisions reflect what patients actually need.

Strengthen

We help clinics improve operations, reimbursement, standards of care and access to specialty services.
