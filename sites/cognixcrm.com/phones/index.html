What's a text segment?

Phone carriers count texts in segments, not messages. One segment is up to 160 letters, numbers and basic symbols. A longer text is split into more segments, and an emoji or special character drops the limit to 70 per segment, so one emoji can double the count. Cognix CRM shows a live counter as you type and warns you when an emoji raises it.

Texting includes 3,750 segments a month shared across your company, then 2¢ a segment. Carriers require a one-time registration of your business before texting turns on. We file it for you; approval usually takes a few days.
