# Tests for the Oct 29 field service build

- `db_stub.sql` + `field_service_test.sql`: run migrations 059 to 062 on a throwaway local Postgres (after the stub,
  which stands in for Supabase's auth, storage and the older CRM tables), then the test walks a job, a timesheet,
  Review decisions, roles, a pay batch, MFA and account separation.
- `fake-supabase.js` + `ui_clickthrough.js`: serve the repo on localhost:8099, then `node tests/ui_clickthrough.js on`
  (or `off`) clicks through every screen against an in-memory fake backend and reports page errors and error toasts.
- `invoices_test.sql`: run with `tests/run_db.sh tests/invoices_test.sql`. Invoice math, numbers, roles, payments through Review, voiding, invoices from jobs and proposals, the customer link, lead source reports.
- `node tests/ui_clickthrough.js docs`: invoices, the invoice editor, Invoices and payments settings, Review payments and the customer page (/doc/) with its print view.
- `members_test.sql`: run with `tests/run_db.sh tests/members_test.sql`. Members of more than one account: adding, switching in, the client changing role, admin and companies, removal, and that other accounts stay invisible.
- `node tests/ui_clickthrough.js members`: the Team screen member card, the member's banner and switcher, and adding from Accounts and Platform.
- `crews_test.sql`: run with `tests/run_db.sh tests/crews_test.sql`. Saved crews, a crew on a job, swaps to Review, missed clock-ins and the foreman's answer, split pay, foreman clocking per service, each person's own steps, schedules with a crew. `node tests/ui_clickthrough.js crews` clicks dispatch (crew menu and drag), the missed clock-in answer, the crew card, Review, saved crews and crew settings.
- `measure_test.sql`: run with `tests/run_db.sh tests/measure_test.sql`. The Measuring switch, the plain starting set, who can see and change prices and types, tap state checks, saving measurements, other accounts kept out, and the map picture on the customer's invoice.
- `node tests/measure_math_test.mjs`: area (with cutouts), length, totals and price lines against shapes with known sizes.
- `node tests/ui_clickthrough.js measure`: draws an area with a cutout, a line and two poles (one tapped again to moved), prices it into a new invoice and a proposal draft, Measure and price inside an invoice, the setup screen and the customer page picture. `fake-leaflet.js` stands in for Leaflet.
- `bids_test.sql`: run with `tests/run_db.sh tests/bids_test.sql`. The Bids switch, scorecard checks, the weighted score math and distance, thresholds and rescoring, overrides to Review (and the switch that skips it), who sees what by role, other accounts kept out, files in the account folder, reports (win rate, won value) and intake by email address and the public upload page.
- `node tests/ui_clickthrough.js bids`: sets up the scorecard, adds a bid with an upload, answers the crew question, saves details (address lookup), overrides with a required note, measures and prices from the bid, turns a won bid into an invoice, approves an override in Review and sends the public upload page.
- `portal_test.sql`: run with `tests/run_db.sh tests/portal_test.sql`. The Client portal switch, the first invite becoming the client's admin, sign up making a portal login and never staff (and the reverse), a portal login being nobody to the CRM tables, each list showing only that organization and its locations, internal and hidden items left out, view only versus act, another client in the same account, another account, the CRM preview, sections and the files default, approvals, removed logins and the switch turned off.
- `node tests/ui_clickthrough.js portal`: the Client portal card (invite, email again, admin, remove, choose what they see), Tools, Admin, Client portal, the Inbox label and every tab of the client's portal page plus the read only preview.
